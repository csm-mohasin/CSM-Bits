## 📖 What is CSM Bits?

**CSM Bits** is a Chromium browser extension that replaces your New Tab page with a glassmorphism dashboard and adds a set of everyday productivity tools that work on every website.

It runs entirely inside your browser. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours. Every feature can be switched on or off individually from its settings page, **CSM Bits Studio**, so you keep only what you need.

It works in **Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave** and other Chromium-based browsers.

---

## 💡 Why It Exists

Most browsers give you a blank tab and leave the rest to a dozen separate extensions: one for the new tab, one for time tracking, one for clipboard history, one for screenshots, one for typing in Bangla. Each asks for its own permissions, and many send your data to a server.

CSM Bits brings these tools together with one design language and one rule: **your data stays on your device.** If you want a backup or multi-device sync, you can connect your **own** Firebase project, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

## 📚 Complete Feature List

### 1. New Tab Page

- **Profile card** with photo, name, badge line, status pill, typewriter-style rotating roles, bio, skill chips, location, and buttons for Portfolio, GitHub, Email and CV
- **Profile editor** opened with the ✏ button: upload a local photo or use an image link, edit every field, or reset to defaults
- **Verified badge** next to your name (optional)
- **Neon or Accent theme** for the card, following either a cyan-purple neon style or your wallpaper's color
- **Card effects**: rotating glowing border, mouse spotlight, tilt and an aurora background, each switchable
- **Live stats strip** showing time spent today, tasks left, clipboard items and snapshots, each linking to its page
- **Clock and date** with a large live clock and full date
- **Time-based greeting** ("Good morning", "Good evening" and so on) with your name, optional
- **Smart search bar** with Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, YouTube or GitHub as the default engine
- **Search bangs**: start a query with `yt`, `gh`, `g`, `b`, `ddg`, `w` (Wikipedia), `maps` or `tr` (Translate); URLs and domains typed in the bar open directly
- **Quick Links** with favicons or emoji icons, adjustable size, gap, shape (rounded, circle, square) and optional labels
- **Link folders** to group links, open them, rename them, take items out, or break a folder apart
- **Right-click link menu** to edit a link, open it in a new tab, or move it out of a folder
- **To-do list** to add, tick off and delete tasks; the remaining count appears in the stats strip
- **Pomodoro timer** with focus and break modes, adjustable lengths, start/pause, reset and skip
- **Bookmarks widget** with your most recent bookmarks
- **History widget** with your recent browsing history
- **Item counts** for history and bookmarks, or hide either widget
- **Quick toolbar** with shortcuts to Firebase Sync, Clipboard and Snapshots, Stats, JSON Tools, CSM Bits Studio, layout editing, lock-all and the wallpaper panel

### 2. Drag-and-Drop Widget Layout

- **Edit mode**: click ✥ on the New Tab page
- **Drag any widget**: Profile, Clock, Search, Links, To-do, Pomodoro, Bookmarks, History
- **Hide and restore**: ✕ hides a widget and a ＋ chip brings it back
- **Grid snapping** to 10px; hold `Alt` to move freely
- **Reset** to the default layout in one click
- **Synced** across devices when Firebase Sync is on

### 3. Wallpaper and Glass Look

- **Preset gradients**: seven built-in backgrounds
- **Your own image** uploaded from your computer (large images are resized automatically)
- **Image link** from any URL
- **GIF wallpaper**: animated GIFs play as your background
- **Video wallpaper**: MP4 or WebM, muted and looped, stored locally in your browser
- **Wallpaper on websites**: optionally show the GIF or video behind websites too (uses more CPU)
- **Wallpaper blur**, **glass opacity** and **glass blur** sliders
- **Wallpaper gallery** that keeps up to 12 images, switchable with one click
- **Slideshow** that rotates wallpapers on every new tab or every N minutes
- **Accent color sync**: the dominant color of your wallpaper is detected automatically and used across the interface; pick a custom color or re-extract at any time

### 4. Visual Effects

- **Parallax wallpaper** that moves gently with your mouse, with adjustable strength and smoothness
- **Auto drift** so the parallax moves slowly on its own, with adjustable speed
- **Glow cursor**, a soft light that follows the pointer
- **Cursor styles**: Glow, Glass Ball, Ring + Dot, or Dot only
- **Cursor tuning**: size, blur, brightness, follow speed and color (accent or custom)
- **Hover growth** over links and buttons
- **Click ripple** on every click
- **Hide the native cursor** (optional)
- **Scope control**: run effects on the New Tab page only, or on websites too

### 5. Website Glass Theme

- **Glass theme on every site** with a translucent, blurred look over your wallpaper
- **Per-site toggle** from the command palette or Side Panel
- **Skip list** of domains where the theme is never applied

### 6. Tab Lock

- **Passcode lock screen**: a glass overlay hides the page until you unlock it
- **Hashed passcode**: only a salted PBKDF2 hash is stored, and it is never synced
- **Lock on tab switch**, with an adjustable delay (0 means instantly)
- **Lock on window focus loss** when you switch to another app
- **Lock on idle** after a chosen time or when the screen locks
- **Lock on browser restart**: tabs start locked after Chrome is closed and reopened
- **Auto-relock timeout**: tabs lock again a set number of minutes after unlocking
- **Site scope**: lock all sites or only a list of sites, plus a "never lock" list
- **Lock the New Tab page** (optional)
- **Protected browser pages**: pages such as `chrome://extensions` need the passcode even when typed directly (the list is editable)
- **Lock-out protection**: repeated wrong passcodes lead to growing wait times
- **Locked settings**: with a passcode set, Tab Lock settings can only be changed after unlocking them (for 10 minutes)
- **Lock now** with `Alt+Shift+L`, the 🔒 button on the New Tab page, or the command palette
- **Change or remove** the passcode from CSM Bits Studio

> **Note:** Tab Lock deters casual snooping. It is not a replacement for an operating-system login, and it cannot stop someone from disabling the extension itself.

### 7. Command Palette

Press `Ctrl+K` (configurable) on any regular web page. It can:

- run **commands**: open CSM Bits Studio, Stats, JSON Formatter, Clipboard, Snapshots, Time Budget settings and Firebase Sync settings
- take a **snapshot**, a **full-page screenshot** or a **single-screen screenshot**
- **toggle** Bangla typing, the website glass theme, the cursor effect, parallax and the selection popup
- **lock all tabs** instantly
- **group tabs by domain**, **close duplicate tabs** and open the Side Panel
- **export or import** your settings
- search your **open tabs**, **bookmarks** and **history**
- work as a **calculator**: type `2*8+3` and press Enter to copy the result
- search Google with whatever you typed
- open a **quick note** for the current site

The palette does not open on `chrome://` pages, the Chrome Web Store or the PDF viewer, because Chrome blocks extensions there.

### 8. Text Selection Popup

Select text on any page to get a small glass toolbar with:

- **Copy**
- **Search** using your chosen engine
- **Translate** into your chosen language (default Bengali)
- **Note**, which saves the text to that site's notes

Each button can be turned off separately.

### 9. Bangla Phonetic Typing

- **Toggle with `Alt+B`** in any text field (the key is configurable)
- **Type Bangla by sound** in English letters: `ami` → আমি, `bangla` → বাংলা, `bhalo` → ভালো
- **Avro-style rules**: `O` for ও/ো, `T Th D Dh N` for ট ঠ ড ঢ ণ, plus `S Sh`, `R`, `ng`, `Ng`, `^`, `:`, `rri`, `kkh`, `gg`, `,,` for hasanta, and automatic ra, ya and ba-phala
- **Bangla digits** (১২৩), optional
- **Dari key**: pressing `.` can type the Bangla full stop `।`
- **Indicator pill**: a small "বাং / EN" badge in text fields that you can click to toggle
- **Smart backspace** steps back one character at a time
- **Safe by design**: it does not work in password, email or number fields
- **Practice box** in CSM Bits Studio

### 10. Time Tracking

- **Site Time Tracker** records the active time you spend on each domain, excluding idle time (the idle threshold is adjustable)
- **Stats page** with daily, weekly and monthly views: total time, number of sites, daily average, comparison with yesterday or the previous period, busiest day and active days
- **Charts and top sites**: a bar chart plus a ranked list of your most-used sites
- **CSV export** and a button to delete all tracker data
- **Local storage only**, keeping the last 120 days
- **Time Budget**: when a site loads, a glass pop-up asks how long you plan to stay (2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 minutes, unlimited, or a custom time)
- **Countdown chip** in the page corner (optional)
- **When time is up**: a pop-up (or a notification if you are on another tab) lets you extend by a few minutes or close the tab
- **Budget scope**: ask on all sites or only listed ones, with a "never ask" list and a reset button

### 11. Clipboard History

- **Auto-saves copied text** from web pages (never from password fields)
- **Search** through your clips
- **Pin** important clips so they are never cleared
- **Open the source page**, copy the text again, copy the link, or delete a clip
- **Clear all except pinned** in one click
- **Adjustable limits** for how many clips to keep and their maximum length

### 12. Page Snapshots

- **One-key snapshot** with `Alt+Shift+S`: saves a small preview image, the page text, its link and your scroll position
- **Also available** from the Side Panel, the palette and the Hub page
- **Search** snapshots by title, link or text
- **Reopen** the page, copy the text or link, or delete a snapshot
- **Adjustable count**: keep 5 to 100 snapshots

### 13. Screenshots

- **Full-page screenshot** with a glass frame, stitched while scrolling
- **Visible-area screenshot** of the current screen only
- **Customization**: solid or transparent background, padding, a browser bar and a glass border
- **Download as PNG** or **copy to clipboard**, and retake in one click

### 14. Side Panel (Tab Manager)

- **Tab list across all windows** with search
- **Group tabs by domain**, **ungroup** them, and **close duplicates** with a count of how many were closed
- **Sessions**: save your current set of tabs and reopen it later in a new window
- **Site notes** that auto-save for the site you are on
- **Per-site glass theme toggle**
- **Quick tools**: full-page screenshot, visible screenshot, snapshot, and links to Stats and Clipboard
- **Bookmarks and History** browsers
- **Firebase Sync setup** right inside the panel

### 15. Tools and Notes

- **JSON Formatter** to format, minify and sort keys (A→Z) with 2-space, 4-space or tab indentation; errors include line and column; paste, copy and clear buttons
- **Quick notes per site**, opened from the palette or selection popup, auto-saved and included in backups
- **CSM Bits Hub**, one page holding your Clipboard History and Snapshots

### 16. Sync and Backup

- **Firebase Sync** (optional) backs up settings, quick links, notes, clipboard history, snapshots, widget layout and time-tracker data to **your own** Firebase project
- **Easy setup**: paste your `firebaseConfig` and the extension picks out the API key and project ID; includes **Test connection** and **Sync now** buttons
- **AES-256 encryption** when you set a passphrase, applied before data leaves your browser
- **Multi-device**: install on another device and enter the same key (and passphrase) to restore everything
- **Export and Import** to a JSON file with a choice of optional parts, plus a full reset

## 🔧 How It Works

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                         Your Browser                     │
│                                                          │
│  New Tab / Settings / Stats pages   ← extension pages    │
│  Content scripts on websites        ← theme, effects,    │
│                                       palette, typing,   │
│                                       Tab Lock overlay   │
│  Service worker (background)        ← time tracking,     │
│                                       Tab Lock state,    │
│                                       sync, snapshots    │
│                                                          │
│  chrome.storage.local + IndexedDB   ← all your data      │
└───────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┘
                                │  optional, off by default
                                ▼
                    Your own Firebase project
                    (AES-256 if a passphrase is set)
```

- **No build step and no backend.** The extension is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on Manifest V3.
- **Settings** live in `chrome.storage.local` and apply instantly everywhere.
- **Video and GIF wallpapers** are stored as files in IndexedDB; the New Tab plays them directly, and websites play them through a small extension frame.
- **Tab Lock** keeps its unlock state in session storage, so closing the browser locks everything again. The passcode is verified by the background worker, never by the web page.
- **Browser pages** such as `chrome://extensions` cannot run extension scripts, so the tab is redirected to a passcode gate first and sent to the real page after you unlock it.

## 📦 Installation

1. Download the latest zip from the **[Releases](../../releases)** page and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` in your browser.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder, the one that contains `manifest.json`.
5. Open a new tab and enjoy.

**Updating:** download the new release, replace the old folder's files, and click the reload icon on the extension's card.

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Open a new tab** to see your glass dashboard.
2. Click **⚙** to change the wallpaper, or **🎛** to open CSM Bits Studio for every setting.
3. Click **✏** on the profile card and enter your own details.
4. Click **✥** to drag the widgets into your preferred layout.
5. Press **`Ctrl+K`** on any web page to try the command palette.
6. Press **`Alt+B`** in a text box and type `ami` to try Bangla typing.
7. *(Optional)* In **CSM Bits Studio → Tab Lock**, set a passcode and turn on Tab Lock.
8. *(Optional)* In **CSM Bits Studio → Firebase Sync**, connect your own Firebase project for backup.

## 🔐 Privacy and Security

**There is no server and no analytics. The developer never receives your data.**

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settings, links, notes, to-dos, layout | Customization | Your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) |
| Wallpaper image, GIF or video | Background | Your browser |
| Time spent per site (domain only) | Time Tracker and Budget | Your browser, last 120 days |
| Copied text and page snapshots | Clipboard History and Snapshots | Your browser; password fields are never saved |
| Tab Lock passcode | Locking tabs | A salted PBKDF2 hash only, never synced |
| History and bookmarks | New Tab widgets and palette search | Read from Chrome on demand, not copied elsewhere |

If you turn on Firebase Sync, data goes only to **your own** Firebase project, and is encrypted with AES-256 if you set a passphrase. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for the full policy.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is CSM Bits really free?**
Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license.

**Does it collect or sell my data?**
No. Everything stays in your browser. The only network use is the optional Firebase Sync, which talks to your own Firebase project.

**Do I need an account or Firebase?**
No. Firebase Sync is entirely optional and off by default.

**Why does it ask for access to all websites?**
The glass theme, command palette, Bangla typing, selection popup and Tab Lock all run on web pages. Each of these can be turned off.

**Is Tab Lock secure?**
It stops casual access by covering the page and requiring a passcode, and it throttles wrong guesses. It is not a replacement for locking your computer, and anyone who can disable the extension can bypass it.

**Why doesn't the command palette open on some pages?**
Chrome does not allow extensions to run on `chrome://` pages, the Chrome Web Store and the built-in PDF viewer.

**Will video wallpapers slow my browser?**
They use some CPU and memory. On the New Tab page this is usually fine; the option to play them behind websites is off by default for that reason. Very strict websites may block the wallpaper frame.

**Which video formats work?**
MP4 (H.264) and WebM, as supported by Chrome.

**I forgot my Tab Lock passcode. What now?**
The passcode cannot be recovered. Remove the extension from `chrome://extensions` and reinstall it, which also clears its settings.

**Does it work in Firefox?**
Not currently. It is built for Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave).

**How do I update or uninstall?**
To update, replace the files with the new release and press reload on `chrome://extensions`. To uninstall, click **Remove** on the extension's card.
